This project analyzes Amazon product listings to understand how discount strategies influence customer engagement, satisfaction, and long-term revenue potential. Using a dataset of over 1,000 products, the study applies statistical tests, A/B experimentation, and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) modeling to evaluate promotional effectiveness, identify high-value product segments, and guide data-driven pricing and marketing decisions. The analysis also incorporates confidence intervals, power analysis, cohort-style LTV segmentation, and adaptive bandit modeling to ensure rigorous and actionable insights.
